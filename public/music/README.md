# City music tracks (optional)

Drop an MP3 here named after the city id and the arena plays it instead of the built-in
procedural theme for that city:

- `shanghai.mp3` — 上海
- `newyork.mp3` — 纽约
- `paris.mp3` — 巴黎
- `scrap.mp3` — 废料城
- `victory.mp3` — 第 1 名的胜利音乐（4–6 秒；没有这个文件时播放内置的合成号角）

Loop-friendly, 1:30–3:00, loudness about −14 LUFS. Prompts for generating them with Suno are
in `docs/music/suno-prompts.md`. Only commit tracks you hold the rights to use commercially
(Suno: a paid plan at the time of generation).
