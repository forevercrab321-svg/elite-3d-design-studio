## 2. 他怎么谈「销售团队」与「招聘销售」

### 2.1 一手引语（按主题）

**招聘与淘汰——"短窗口"**（MFM 2022-03-15，C6）【一手·转录】
- "I actually have a very short window for allowing people to fail at sales."
- "I've never had a killer salesperson who didn't do pretty well the first week."
- "We turn through it quickly, and as a result the team is just killers."
- "I love this quote from Grant Cardone: 'My sales team's a dangerous place to work.'"（他明确标注为引用 Cardone）

**天生 vs 可训练**
- The Game "Selecting Exceptional Salespeople"：简介转述其观点 "the best salespeople are born in my opinion, not made."【一手·摘，C15，未听原音频】
- X："Growth hack: word your problems as personal deficiencies. 'My sales people suck' VS 'I don't know how to hire train and manage great salespeople.' If you can improve it, it's a skill. If it's a skill, you can learn it. But you have to admit you suck before it can get better."【一手·摘，C16】

**线索分配与团队结构**（YouTube "Ultimate Sales Training 2026"，C1）【一手·转录】
- "Feed the killers … the best leads should go to the best closers and the worst leads should go to the worst closers"
- "Your best closer usually isn't your sales manager … typically the best closers are very like Hunter type person"
- "If your top sales rep can make a million dollar a year … do you know the amount of people who will work for nothing for the opportunity"（用顶尖销售的收入上限做招聘磁铁）
- "The whole idea that people think that the lead quality is go down is not really true it's completely dependent on how well you work the leads"
- "There's a lot of superstition that starts to happen in sales … they end up longterm actually eroding their ability to sell"

**顶尖销售的行为清单**（YouTube "9 Concepts"，C4）【一手·转录】
- 分类比喻："there's three types of sales people there's dogs horses and tigers"——注意原文是 "he said"，**他在转述别人的分类**，非原创。
- 行为：拥有最多可用工时；主动"pull up calls"（有预约就提前拉上来）；"they don't take rejection personally"；"they have something called a kill list"；"they create their own opportunities"；"the best salespeople listen twice as much as they talk"；"they have key stories or metaphors to break the belief"；"kill zombies up front now that's the term that we use internally"。

**训练方法**
- "record all your sales … when you get on that hot streak and then you get cold again watch your hot streak"（C2）【一手·转录】
- "report all your sales … it's also the easiest way to train new people"（C3）【一手·转录】
- "The most productive sales people … ask for the sale more times than anyone else."（C5）【一手·转录】
- "Conviction is the number one predictor of good sales." / "Trust is the thing that transfers that conviction."（C6）【一手·转录】
- "the process of filling conviction is one of the easiest and best ways to increase someone's closing percentage better than any of the training in the world"（C2）【一手·转录】
- 销售者的前提："they have to be comfortable with the no and they have to be comfortable with the gasp"（C2）

**销售的层级观**
- X："Level 1 salesmen focus on getting better at sales. Level 2 salesmen focus on getting better people to sell to. Level 3 salesmen focus on getting better things to sell. Level 4 salesmen don't sell anything at all and simply make it easy for people to buy."【一手·摘，C17】

**规模化招聘**（二手摘要，C20）
- Ep 823：借助专门的销售猎头，两周内加了 40 名销售（二手转述）。
- Ep 884：服务型业务建议内部销售团队，前端 closer 与后端 upsell 销售之间做交接（二手转述）。

### 2.2 立场变化的瞬间（重要）

- **2022（MFM）**：强调淘汰速度，认可 Cardone 的 "dangerous place to work"。
- **2025–2026（YouTube 销售培训）**："I used to be really hardcore and Cutthroat … I do but in a different way so I don't want it to be us versus ourselves"（C1）【一手·转录】——他自述从"内部厮杀式"竞争转向"不是我们自己对自己"的方式；同一视频还说 "I think competition is very good but not in the way that you think it is"。
- 另一处自我修正：他提到自己团队出现过"absurd"的 100% 成交率，结果发现是内部统计口径问题（原文转写不完整："it was we out day"，含义不清，**不做解读**）。

### 2.3 矛盾（原样记录）

1. **"最好的销售是天生的" vs "如果能改进就是技能，承认自己不会招/训/管"**，外加他以"教会 115+ 名销售"作为节目卖点。三者并存。一种可能解释是"选人靠天赋、方法靠训练"，但**这是我的推断，他本人没这样调和过**（至少我没查到）。
2. **"sales team is a dangerous place to work"（2022）vs "I used to be really hardcore and cutthroat"（2025–26）**：时间上看是立场演变，而非同期矛盾。

## 3. 他怎么谈「定价」

【一手·转录】除特别注明外。
- 锚定："when you say $100,000 first a grand feels like a rounding error"（C2/C3）
- 心跳测试："if you didn't get a gasp from a price tag you didn't go high enough this is another belief"（C2）——注意他自己称之为 "belief"。
- 付款方式而非买不买："the question is not whether you're going to buy it or not it's just how do you want to pay"（C2）
- 机会成本框架："you're going to spend this money either way over the next 12 months"（C2）
- 数学："If 10% of people buy something that's 10 times expensive, you double your revenue."（C9）
- 获客自付："Gross profit in 30 days is greater than two times CAC plus COGS."（C9）
- 降价实验："I cut the top-line price by 25% and saw absolutely no change in churn"（C7，MFM 2022；上下文未完整获取，不确定他是用来论证什么）
- 连续订阅价："Forty-two grand a year"（C7，指其 Legacy 类持续项目定价，上下文有限）
- 渠道价值差异（MFM 连线 2025，C8）："Your TikTok views are not worth nearly what your Instagram views are. It's probably a factor of 10 to 20x difference in value per view."
- 客户质量："You have to have certain types of customers. If you have shitty customers, they're going to treat you shitty."（C8）

## 4. 他怎么谈「免费 vs 付费」

- "Make your free shit better than their paid shit."（X，2022-05-26）【一手·摘】
- "Giveaway the secrets, sell the implementation."（X，2022 起多次；也在 TikTok 与 Ep 523 宣传中重复："I've said it before, give away the secrets, sell the implementation."）【一手·摘】
- LinkedIn："If you're starting out, give your stuff away for free. When people say…"（截断）【一手·摘】
- 免费的边界（MFM 2022，C7）：谈早期 Gym Launch 时 "You didn't pay anything, so best of luck"——语境是对**没付钱的人**不承担同等交付责任【一手·转录，语境片段】。
- 试用去风险（C3）："i'm not even asking you to make a decision right now … i didn't fulfill my promise … i'll give you the money back"【一手·转录】
- 课程（见 01 文件）："The best way for someone to know that they're going to get value from you when they pay you is to give them value before they pay you."
- 风险阶梯（MFM 连线 2025，C8）："You have the luxury of being able to test product-market fit before doing anything. You can affiliate someone else's thing, see what the demand looks like."

【推断】他的"免费"不是慈善，而是**销售工具**（他自己的原话 "you're selling or giving away a selling tool"）：免费内容负责建立信任与揭示更大的问题，付费负责执行与速度。

## 5. 被追问时怎么回答：即兴类比库

【一手·转录】
- 卖结果："Sell them when they're excited about the bikini, not about the TSA line to Maui"（C7）——CLOSER 中 "sell the vacation" 的口语版。
- 理性地基："it's like dating … if you have a rational foundation for a relationship or a decision when the emotions fade the logic will continue"（C2/C3）
- 被坏教练伤过的客户："it's like having a bad eighth grade boyfriend or girlfriend and being like you know what men aren't for me all coaches suck"（C2）
- 诊断先于回答："Hey you're asking me what's wrong with your car and it would be unethical for me to answer it without looking under the hood first."（C5）
- 谁来回答："It would be like you asking the secretary about how your heart surgery is going to be with the surgeon"（C5）
- 控制权："the person who ask the questions is the one who's in control"；"No one can disagree with a question … be like smoke so you can't catch smoke"（C5）
- 成交率与结果："the top five red zone offenses in the league … made the playoffs 90 of the time"（C3，NFL 红区类比）
- 捆绑必需品："You can't have X without Y, right? You can't have a burger without fries."（C9，Classic Upsell）
- 时机："You want to sell at the point of greatest deprivation, not the point of greatest value."（C9）
- 季节："just like winter feels different than summer"（C10，谈人生阶段）
- 顶尖销售的纠缠力："you're like a Pit Bull on someone's ankle"（C4，转述他人对他的评价）

**应答风格（从逐字稿观察）【推断，基于一手材料】**
1. 被问"怎么办"时先改写问题（"The contingency thing — the question is whether it's a feature or a bug"，C8）。
2. 大量用数字给出判断（"23% isn't terrible … that's just a normal close rate"，C8）。
3. 直接给处方且极具体（"You're missing a VSL. That's all … You're going to write a seven-minute VSL."，C8）。
4. 对异议不对抗，改成好奇："You can sell without ever having confrontation with what I call childlike curiosity." / "That's crazy—tell me more about that."（C6）
5. 在销售培训里语言粗粝，偶有冒犯性比喻（C2 中 "closing is a dance not a fight" 后面接了一个性暴力比喻）——**记录为事实，Skill 不应复制这类措辞**。

## 6. 承认错误 / 改变立场

【一手·转录】
- **ALAN 选错产品**（MFM 2025，C9）："I should have built a CRM for gyms … That was probably a $300 million miss … I literally allocated capital to build technology and I built the wrong one."（转写原文把 ALAN 写成 "Allen"）
- **组合投资的反思**（C9）："I would have been better served doing less … if I had just kept it simple, picked one of those businesses or built one one great business, I could have done better than I did spreading my focus."
- **Gym Launch 的回头客**（C2/C3）："I learned that second half the hard way because we found out in gym launch that people would say no sometimes to buying memberships"——之后加入"允许他们回来"的机制而卖出更多。
- **卖掉公司后的失落**（MFM 2022，C7）："I looked back on the times when I was building those companies with nostalgia" / "That was some of the happiest times of my life. And now I'm bored"
- **驱动力来源**（MFM 2022，C10）："I think originally the drive came from just crippling insecurity and needing approval." / "I have this massive pile of money that I use as an emotional crutch to tell myself I'm not a piece of shit."
- **销售团队文化**：见 §2.2。

## 10. 对游戏销售团队的可迁移点（全部为【推断】）

背景：GROW EVERYTHING——浏览器 3D 多人 .io 游戏；收入＝外观内购、VIP 会员、激励广告、平台分成（CrazyGames/Poki）。

1. **【推断】"Feed the killers" → 把最好的"线索"给最能转化的触点。** 对游戏而言"线索"是高意向玩家时刻（刚升到高段位、刚被击杀、刚解锁新区域）。最好的商品位、最强的限时礼包应只出现在这些时刻，而不是均匀铺在所有界面。
2. **【推断】"Level 4 salesmen … simply make it easy for people to buy" 是游戏内购的第一原则。** 优先工作是减少购买步骤、在局内直接预览外观、平台支付一步完成，而不是写更多促销文案。
3. **【推断】平台 BD（CrazyGames/Poki 谈判）套用 CLOSER：** Clarify 平台要什么（留存、时长、广告填充率）→ Label 他们的问题（同类 .io 游戏留存低）→ Overview 他们过去试过什么 → Sell the vacation（上线后的曲线与数据，不是技术细节）→ Explain away concerns（性能、包体、合规）→ Reinforce（上线后第一周主动报数据）。
4. **【推断】他的 MFM 观点"不同渠道每次曝光价值差 10–20 倍"提醒我们：** 按平台/渠道分别计算 ARPDAU 与广告 eCPM，不要把 CrazyGames、Poki、自有站的流量混算定价或投放。
5. **【推断】他从"hardcore and cutthroat"到"不是我们自己对自己"的转变：** 小团队的销售/运营岗位应以共享指标（整体 LTV、留存）为主，避免内部抢流量位的零和 KPI。
6. **【推断】"You want to sell at the point of greatest deprivation"＋他的 "comfortable with the gasp"：** 可以测试一个高锚点商品（高价限定外观）放在商店首位，让常规皮肤价格显得合理（Anchor Upsell）；但所有"匮乏时刻"的变现都要用 A/B 数据验证，避免伤害留存——对平台分成模式而言，留存本身就是收入。
