## 6. Recurring principles (appear in ≥2 independent sources)

1. **Playable evidence beats pitch prose.** Raw Fury (1.4), Devolver (1.5), Kwalee 1–3-level CPI builds (2.6), Supersonic MVP (2.5), CrazyGames Basic Launch (3.3). The buyer wants to *measure or feel* the game.
2. **Cheap, fast, numeric first gate; kill quickly.** Voodoo (2.1), Raw Fury "say no fast" (1.1), Supersonic/Kwalee CPI tests (2.5–2.6), CrazyGames 7-day/500-play gate (3.3).
3. **Benchmarks are the buyer's own, relative, and moving.** vectraplay on Voodoo (2.4), CrazyGames "benchmarked against other games" (3.3), divergent CPI/retention bars (2.3, 2.5, 2.7).
4. **Standardise intake.** Raw Fury template deck (1.3), Devolver pitch form (1.6), Homa Lab/SDK (2.8), CrazyGames/GameDistribution portals & SDKs (3.3, 3.6).
5. **Named owner per account/genre; portfolio management.** Poki AM portfolio (3.1), SayGames genre owner (2.9), Homa publishing managers (2.8), Roblox vertical partner managers (3.7).
6. **Relationships are personal and slow.** Lemkin 2× cycle & in-person (4.1–4.2), GameDistribution "trust and transparency" (3.5), NetEase team wipe-out showing relationships live with people (1.9) [partly inferred].
7. **Multi-thread and make the buyer commit to actions.** Gong (4.8), MAP-as-evidence (4.9), Roblox partner launches coordinated across many internal teams (3.7).
8. **Every role owns a number; attribution explicit.** Crossbeam KPIs (4.5), forecastable failure modes (4.6), Poki AM "grow your portfolio" (3.1).
9. **Learn from losses with buyer-side evidence.** Clozd 15% alignment (4.10), Supersonic "why your prototype failed" iteration posts (2.5).

## 7. Contradictions kept

- Voodoo D1/D7 bars: 55/22 vs 40/10 (2.3). CPI target: <$0.25 (CODA) vs <$0.35 (Supersonic) vs "relative to our recent tests" (vectraplay).
- Partnerships early: Lemkin "almost always work, but slowly; rarely the first $1M" vs Liao "make no sense at seed" (4.1 vs 4.3). For a studio selling *to* platforms, the platform *is* the channel, so the Liao warning applies to *secondary* partnerships, not to portal distribution itself [inferred].
- NetEase global commitment vs 2024 layoffs (1.9–1.10).
- Win/loss vendor claims (Gartner 50%) are relayed by vendors with a commercial interest.

## 8. Proposed org chart — 1 founder + AI agents ToB team  **[SYNTHESIS — my design, not from any source]**

```text
Founder = Head of BD / deal owner (only human; signs, negotiates, attends meetings)
│
├── A. Scout & Qualifier agent        (platform research, ICP fit, contact mapping)
├── B. Packager agent                  (pitch kit, builds, metadata, per-platform compliance)
├── C. Outreach & Cadence agent        (drafts first touches, follow-ups, meeting requests)
├── D. Deal Desk agent                 (terms comparison, rev-share/exclusivity math, red-flags)
├── E. Partner Success agent           (post-launch metrics, platform-AM updates, next asks)
└── F. Pipeline Ops & Win/Loss agent   (CRM hygiene, weekly review pack, loss post-mortems)
```

| Role | Borrowed from | Weekly outputs | KPIs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder (Head of BD) | Poki AM / Raw Fury scout / Lemkin "in person" | ≥3 personalised touches to top-tier platforms; all live negotiations; approves every outbound; runs 30-min Monday pipeline review | # platforms at "live" stage; revenue per platform; # multi-threaded accounts (≥2 contacts) |
| A. Scout & Qualifier | Raw Fury "fast no", Devolver fit/capacity check | Updated platform dossier (submission rules, rev-share, exclusivity, audience, contact names); fit score per platform; 1 new candidate/week once first 10 are in play | Dossiers current (<30 days old); % of pursued platforms that pass fit (target set by founder) |
| B. Packager | Kwalee/Supersonic "minimal testable build", Raw Fury template deck | Per-platform build + SDK checklist status; pitch one-pager; trailer/GIF; metadata pack | Submission-ready kits; QA rejections per submission (goal: trending to 0); time from "go" to submitted |
| C. Outreach & Cadence | MeetToMatch "materials in first message", MAP | Drafts for all due follow-ups; conference meeting requests (start ~4 weeks before event); MAP draft for every active deal | Reply rate; meetings booked; % active deals with buyer-acknowledged next step + date |
| D. Deal Desk | Deal-desk (complexity trigger), Epic/Poki term structures | Side-by-side term sheet for each offer; flags on exclusivity, IP, payment thresholds, termination | Zero unreviewed non-standard terms signed; decision turnaround ≤48h |
| E. Partner Success | Poki AM roadmap, CrazyGames Basic→Full metrics, Homa LaunchOps | Per-platform weekly metric card (plays, avg playtime, conversion-to-gameplay, retention, revenue); one "what we'll ship next" note to each platform AM | Promotion to next platform tier (e.g. Basic→Full Launch); featured placements; metric deltas week-on-week |
| F. Pipeline Ops & Win/Loss | Crossbeam attribution, Clozd win/loss, Voodoo kill discipline | CRM clean-up; pipeline snapshot; 1 win/loss note per closed or stalled deal (ask the platform "why" in their words) | CRM completeness; stage-age alerts; # loss reasons captured *from the buyer* |

**Minimum CRM fields [synthesis]:** platform · tier (A/B/C) · model (rev-share / licence / exclusive / self-serve) · stage (Research → Qualified → Contacted → Conversation → Submitted → In review → Soft/Basic launch → Full/featured → Expanding | Lost/Paused) · contacts (≥2, with role) · champion · next step + buyer-named date · MAP link · required tech (SDK, ads, size limits) · key terms · platform metrics snapshot · loss reason (buyer's words) · last touch date.

**Cadence [synthesis]:** Mon pipeline review (F prepares, founder decides kill/push); Wed outreach batch (C); Fri partner-metrics cards (E) and dossier refresh (A). Monthly: win/loss review + benchmark update. Per conference: requests out T-4 weeks, materials in first message, follow-up within 48h.

**Human-only boundaries [synthesis]:** agents draft, founder sends; no agent signs, agrees terms, or impersonates the founder in live meetings; platform contacts must be told they are dealing with the studio (disclosure on AI-drafted material is the founder's call per platform norms).
